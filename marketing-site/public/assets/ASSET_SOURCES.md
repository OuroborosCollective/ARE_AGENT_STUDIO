# ARE Signal Control Room assets

These raster assets were generated for ARE Agent Studio's Signal Control Room visual system and are included locally so the website, studio UI, and Hugging Face presentation can share one art direction without hot-linking a transient generator URL.

- `are-signal-hero.png` — hero/control-room backdrop
- `observe-frame.png` — observation step image
- `human-correction.png` — human-correction step image
- `device-readback.png` — device-readback step image

They are visual presentation assets only. They do not depict, certify, or add runtime evidence.
